Kingsman: The Secret Service

Directed by Matthew Vaughn

Manners Maketh Man.

Chapter 1

Eggsy is introduced as quick-witted and capable. Although, he is also shown to be held back by his environment and how often he chooses to sabotage himself.

Chapter 2

Eggsy triggers the emergency contact his father left. All of a sudden it feels like there is an answer for him, showing hope underneath it all.

Chapter 3

Galahad steps into Eggsy's life and tests his composure. He reveals that Kingsman are serious agents teetering between life and death.

Chapter 4

Eggsy is thrown "straight to the wolves." He begins aiming for something higher than just surviving, navigating a world of new rules.
